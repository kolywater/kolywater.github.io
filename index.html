2015 - 2020

Leadership and team building.

 

From 2015 to 2020, I worked as a product leader and strategist, focused on building and leading great products and teams. Directing product at Ginger.io, leading experience design at Samsung R&D, and finally directing design for Livongo’s all-new mental health app.

Mastering product

Before leading and growing teams, I honed my craft making exceptional product experiences from the ground up.
